Scoring towns...
Only towns with 10+ closed sales that had a price cut appear — thin samples are excluded rather than shown as noise. Cut-rates are shrunk toward the county average so a small town can't post a false edge. Score is an editorial blend of the measured stats, not a prediction.

Scoring live deals...

MOMLS MARKET LEADERBOARDS
⚠ Agent rankings are in verification beta — figures are provisional until agents verify their profiles. Rankings reflect MLS-recorded transaction sides only; co-agented sides are split 50/50.
Computing rankings...
Methodology. Rankings are computed solely from MLS-recorded closed transactions in Monmouth & Ocean County, NJ, credited to the listing and buyer agents/offices of record. Agent credit: each transaction contributes one listing side and one buyer side; an agent recorded on a side receives 1.0 credited side and 100% of that side's volume — unless a co-listing or co-buyer agent is recorded, in which case each co-agent receives 0.5 credited side and 50% of the volume. An agent recorded on both sides of the same transaction is credited both sides ("double-ended"). Brokerage attribution: the canonical brokerage ranking entity is the MLS Office ID (the branch office of record), so independent brokerages are compared against equivalent operating units; same-name branches are shown with their office number. "Brand networks" is a supplemental, name-based rollup and is not the canonical ranking. Exclusions: recorded closings outside a $50K–$25M sanity band, or closing more than 3× above / 4× below final asking, are excluded as likely data-entry errors; out-of-MLS ("non-member") sides are excluded because the MLS records them under a placeholder identity, not a licensed member of record. Verification: "✓ Profile verified" means MOMLS matched the claimant to the MLS identity of record; it is not an MLS endorsement, award, or statement of quality. Terminology: rankings state exactly what they measure — e.g. "#1 by closed volume" — and are never a claim of being the "best" or "top-performing" professional. Rankings are provisional and may change as attribution is corrected, records are normalized, additional transactions are ingested, and claims are verified. No payment affects rankings.
NJ PUBLIC RECORDS — RECORDED HOME SALES
Every recorded, arm's-length residential deed in New Jersey — all 21 counties, 500+ municipalities — from the State's official SR-1A sales records. Independent of any MLS.
Loading recorded sales...
Source & method. NJ Division of Taxation SR-1A deed records (official state data, updated periodically; recording typically lags closing by 1–3 months). Only sales the State classifies as usable / arm's-length are counted — family transfers, sheriff's deeds, and other non-market conveyances are excluded by the State's own coding. "Residential" = property class 2 (1–4 family). YoY compares 2026 year-to-date with the same months of 2025. This data is independent of, and unaffiliated with, any MLS.
